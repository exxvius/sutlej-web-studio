About FURN HEAVEN

In 1980, Pusaram Jangid laid the foundation of Furn Heaven with a vision to create furniture that reflects tradition, trust, and craftsmanship. From those humble beginnings, Furn Heaven has grown into one of the most admired furniture manufacturers in Jodhpur, recognized for its timeless designs and unmatched quality.

Carrying forward this legacy, Vishnu Jangid, son of Pusaram Jangid, expanded Furn Heaven’s journey with passion and dedication. His focus on fine handicraft furniture has helped us preserve the rich heritage of Jodhpur while blending it with innovative designs that meet the needs of modern homes.

At Furn Heaven, every piece we create is more than furniture – it’s a masterpiece of artistry, skill, and tradition. Our team of expert artisans combines age-old techniques with modern innovation to craft furniture that is elegant, durable, and truly unique. This balance is what makes us stand apart as leading furniture manufacturers in Jodhpur.
