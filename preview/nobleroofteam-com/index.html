About Roofing Repair and Replacement Services in East Texas

Noble Roofing began in East Texas, serving communities like Kilgore, Henderson, Longview, and Tyler with a focus on quality and care. As we grew, we expanded to include our West Texas Branch, establishing dedicated roots in both Lubbock and Amarillo. Each location is highly supported by it’s own dedicated team of caring, highly experienced, trained and certified commercial & residential roofing professionals.

Whether it’s a new installation, a new metal roof, or a full asphalt roof replacement, our family-owned company continues to provide trusted residential and commercial roofing solutions across East & West Texas evident by our high customer satisfaction rating.

Give us a call for your roofing services needs, hail damage inspections, & flat or commercial roofing needs. We handle it all from roof repair to full roof replacement and installation services and are always glad to stop by and provide a second storm or hail damage inspection or roof repair, or new roof opinion. As always, our inspections are free.
